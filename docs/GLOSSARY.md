# Glossary

Business and product terms used across the codebase, content, and copy. Keep terminology consistent — see rules below before writing any customer-facing copy.

## Mandatory Terminology

| Use | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Umrah | Umroh | Standardized spelling across all product surfaces. |
| Jemaah | Jamaah | Standardized spelling across all product surfaces. |
| Pembimbing | Muthowif / Mutawif | Standardized term for the guide/companion role. Always note that the guide included in the base package covers **1x pelaksanaan umrah** only — additional guiding is a paid add-on. |

## Product & Business Terms

- **Umrah Mandiri / Umrah Sendiri** — self-arranged Umrah, where the traveler books flights independently and buys only the land arrangement (LA) from us.
- **LA (Land Arrangement)** — the in-Saudi package: hotel, transport, visa, guide, meals (excludes flights).
- **LPP (Land Package Private)** — the customer-facing priced document/page for an LA package.
- **Backoffice** — internal cost/markup/margin tracking, never shown to customers.
- **Okupansi** — room occupancy basis for pricing: Sendiri (1 pax), Berdua (2 pax), Bertiga (3 pax), Berempat (4 pax).
- **Tanpa Hotel** — LA package for jemaah who already have their own Makkah/Madinah accommodation; excludes hotel and meals (meals are a hotel-tied facility).
- **Add-On** — optional paid services purchased separately from the base LA price (e.g. Handling Bandara, Pembimbing Tambahan, Transport Jabal Khandamah, City Tour, Visa Umrah stand-alone).
- **Siskopatuh** — mandatory Saudi Umrah registration system; bundled into "Paket Dokumen Wajib" together with visa and health insurance.
