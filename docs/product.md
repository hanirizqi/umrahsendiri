# Product

## Vision
Become Indonesia's most trusted independent Umrah planning platform.

## Mission
Help Indonesian Muslims confidently plan Umrah without relying on traditional package tours.

## Value Proposition
- Independent planning
- Transparent costs
- Trusted guidance
- Practical tools
- Educational content

## Success Metrics
- Organic traffic
- Qualified WhatsApp inquiries
- Consultation bookings
- Returning visitors
- User satisfaction

## Product Principles
- Simplicity over complexity
- Trust over hype
- Long-term value over short-term conversion

## Product Lines

We sell Land Arrangement (LA) only — no flights. Two product families, priced per jemaah by room occupancy (Sendiri/Berdua/Bertiga/Berempat):

- **Dengan Hotel** — Bintang 3 / Bintang 4 / Bintang 5 tiers, 9-day program (4 nights Makkah, 3 nights Madinah). Includes transport, meals, hotel, mandatory documents (visa/Siskopatuh/insurance), and a guide (1x umrah execution).
- **Tanpa Hotel** — for jemaah with their own accommodation. Same base facilities minus hotel and meals.
- **Add-On** — optional paid extras: airport handling, extra guide days, Jabal Khandamah transport, Makkah city tour, stand-alone visa processing for group bookings.

Current public prices, hotel details, and full inclusion/exclusion lists: `docs/PRICING.md`. Terminology: `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.
